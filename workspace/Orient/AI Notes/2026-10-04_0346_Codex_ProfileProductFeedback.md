## Proposed direction
- Position around an actionable, user-controlled AI collaboration profile with a visible improvement on a real user task. Treat demand, efficacy, willingness to pay and repeat use as hypotheses.
- Retain engaging progression and a live visual profile, but describe measured estimates and evidence rather than an exact reconstruction of psychology. Distinguish explicit preferences from inferred traits and observed behavior. Any displayed confidence must be justified by the measurement model.
- Consider whether immediate trait labels steer later answers; test that risk and separate progress feedback from detailed interpretation where appropriate. Reward participation and discovery rather than supposedly superior personality traits.
- Proposed initial audience: adults using AI to learn technical skills. This is an initial customer hypothesis, not a market-size or demand finding.
- A compact assessment, reviewable profile, paired response demonstration and usable export could test the value. A mobile-friendly web app could serve desktop and phone users initially; native apps and broader integrations can follow demonstrated use.
- Headless cloud operation can use on-demand model calls and background jobs. A versioned assessment/scoring component should remain distinct from AI explanations and conversational delivery. No dedicated always-running agent per user is required by this concept.
- Exporting a profile does not grant access to future conversations in external AI tools. Continuous refinement needs consented feedback, in-product tasks or supported integrations. Do not promise automatic universal synchronization.
- Recommend sharing concise actionable collaboration guidance under user control and keeping raw answers private by default. Test design and validation warrant psychometric expertise.
- A free introductory experience and paid deeper profile/export are coherent hypotheses. Subscription value needs recurring utility, such as useful refinements or supported integrations; a static export alone does not establish it.
- Compare against a strong simple alternative: an existing assistant collecting explicit preferences. A compelling profile description alone does not prove useful personalization.
